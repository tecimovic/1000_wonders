# GTFS Realtime

The MBTA General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) Realtime feeds provide trip updates, vehicle positions, and live service alerts, updated several times per day. [GTFS](/developers/gtfs) and GTFS Realtime data describe our system and service, in an industry-standard format. To learn more, visit GTFS.org:

[Read GTFS Realtime Reference documentation](https://gtfs.org/documentation/realtime/reference/)
